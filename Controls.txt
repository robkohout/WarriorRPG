Move - ASDW
Look - Mouse
Equip / Unequip - 1
Roll - Move + Spacebar
PickUp - E
Light Attack - LMB (Left Mouse Button)
Heavy Attack - RMB (Right Mouse Button) 
Light Special Attack - Left Alt + LMB
Heavy Special Attack - Left Alt + RMB
Combos - 
LMB LMB LMB LMB 
LMB LMB LMB RMB 
RMB RMB
Block - Left Shift
Parry - Left Shift (When attack arrives)
Counter Attack - 
Parry + LMB
Parry + RMB
Rage Mode - Left Ctrl (When bar is full)
Target Lock - Middle Mouse Button OR Thumb Mouse Button
Change Lock Target - Mouse Move Left or Right
Pause - Escape
